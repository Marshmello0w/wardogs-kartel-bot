Bakurani: mountains, industry and a moving prize

Published descriptions identify Bakurani as a factory district surrounded by Eastern European mountains. That is confirmed map information, not a POI name invented by a keyboard cat.

Up to 100 players split between three teams in WARDOGS. The battlefield covers 256 km², while a randomised 2 × 2 km Control Zone awards points; the first team to 100 wins the treat.

Artillery catculations on Bakurani

Place the gun and target with a click or enter coordinates. The catculator returns distance, azimuth, coordinate differences and the selected weapon solution.

Bakurani tiles, coordinate search, saved targets and tactical scribbles use the same calibrated reference.

L81 Mortar and SPH-2 on Bakurani

The L81 Mortar shows firing-table MIL, distance, azimuth and range status. SPH-2 LOW and HIGH solutions do their cat math separately.

Weapon, active gun, target and range circle stay with each player and never sit on a teammate’s firing solution.

Bakurani terrain and Terrain3D

Bakurani includes contours and Terrain3D elevation data. The normal table value stays visible beside the experimental candidate.

Terrain3D sleeps by default. Only SAFE candidates wake it; uncertain, unsupported or unreachable results use the normal firing table. Platform and chassis tilt are not catculated.

Team planning on the same Bakurani meowp

Create a lobby and share its invite link or code. Drawings, zones, polygons and user markers synchronise between connected cats and humans.

Teammates see labelled player positions but not somebody else’s range circle. The lobby synchronises browser planning; it does not chase live game-server data.

Bakurani map paws

Ruler measures, Pencil scribbles, and Zone or Polygon fences an area. Tactical markers, Eraser, Undo and Redo are also ready under the paw.

Map-tool data can be imported and exported separately from saved targets.

Bakurani Meowp FAQ

How do I open Bakurani in the catculator?

Choose “Open Bakurani Meowp”. The catculator starts with a validated map parameter and remembers the selection normally.

Which big tubes work on Bakurani?

The L81 Mortar firing table and SPH-2 LOW/HIGH solutions are supported.

Can the squad plan together on Bakurani?

Yes. The lobby shares drawings, zones, polygons and tactical markers while weapon, gun, target and range circle remain personal.

Must Terrain3D be enabled?

No. It is an experimental option that sleeps by default. The normal firing table remains the safe fallback.
