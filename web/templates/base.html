Cookies and Steam sign-in

We use a required, secure session cookie to protect forms and complete Steam OpenID sign-in. It expires when you close your browser.

Choose “Accept and stay signed in” if you want us to remember your Steam sign-in on this device for up to 12 hours. Rejecting does not block Steam sign-in; it only keeps the login temporary.

Rejecting does not save your choice, profile, or Steam login beyond the current browser session. To count unique visits, the portal keeps a server-side keyed identifier derived from your IP address; no raw IP address is stored. This portal does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
